# S9.5 — Immersive Japanese Lab / Final QA

Baseline: LatSol-JFT-JLS-S9.4-Mega-Final-Clean.zip (182 files). Development checkpoint, NOT Production Stable.

## Scope and design decisions
- M0 architecture: separate `immersive-engine.js`, `immersive-ui.js`, `curriculum/s95-immersive.js`.
- M1–M6: eight fictional scenarios (housing, shopping, restaurant, transit, workplace, clinic, administration, emergency). No official procedural, medical or emergency advice. Each contains one reading, one listening, one independent dictation phrase, two branch-response questions and one self-practice shadowing/roleplay card. Branch starts with an ungraded choice of goal and leads to one scored follow-up, not a multi-turn free conversation. F1–F5; no F0 scenario. Chapters 05, 07, 09, 12, 13, 14 have scenario coverage; chapter links elsewhere open the full catalog.
- M7: TTS playback at 0.75/0.9/1.0; listening and dictation require audio completion before submission. No automatic speech recognition, recording or pronunciation score. Japanese, kana, romaji and Indonesian meanings shown in feedback. Reading and dialogue prompts are visible before answering. Choice answers use authored keys, dictation allows exact authored Japanese and kana variants after normalization; no semantic AI grader.
- M8: new per-profile key `latsoljft-learning-s95:<encoded profile>` under existing Unified Storage allowlist. Objective mastery: three correct answers on separate UTC dates, review 1/3/7/14/30 days; incorrect resets streak and schedules ten-minute review. Speaking confirmation is separate activity evidence, repeated after seven days. Adaptive Path 3.0 registers 48 rows (40 mastery, 8 practice); stage/prerequisite logic unchanged, scenario selection from BAB and adaptive dashboard. Filter due/weak within selected scenario. No changes to legacy stored records.
- M9: regression, integrity, asset and coverage checks. No file removed. Cache version `jls-s95-1`; added three assets. Old cache-version test assertions updated only to new version.

## Content and limitations
Structural audit checks eight scenario IDs, valid chapter/stage, 48 non-duplicate primary texts within the new catalog, required language/feedback fields and distinct answer options. Authored reading and translation reviewed during drafting, no independent Japanese educator or formal proficiency calibration. Common expressions may recur across modules. One question per reading/listening, one dictation and one follow-up per branch; mastery is repeated-card performance, not transfer proficiency. Speaking is self-confirmation only, optional TTS for shadowing; no pronunciation scoring. TTS requires an installed Japanese voice; no prerecorded audio or offline audio guarantee. No new independent exam bank, no CBT changes. Keep prior ZIP for rollback and back up progress before deployment. Do not claim production stable.
