# S7.1 — Verified Curriculum Architecture / verification ledger

## Verified with official sources (23 November 2026)
- Japan Foundation Irodori Starter / Elementary 1 / Elementary 2 official lesson listings: **54/54 lesson titles**, with volume and lesson numbers. Source links are attached to each entry in `curriculum/s71-architecture.json`.
- Starter Lesson 1 official English table of contents: **4 selected activity Can-do statements** inspected and paraphrased in Indonesian. This is **not** verification of all activities or of the other 53 lessons.
- 3A Network: Minna no Nihongo Shokyu second edition has **25+25=50 lessons**; the publisher describes a structured sentence-pattern learning sequence. **The specific grammar objective and sequence within each chapter have NOT been verified**, so no per-chapter alignment is asserted.

## Explicitly unverified
- Can-do for the remaining Irodori lessons and all uninspected activities.
- Minna second-edition per-chapter grammar contents and detailed order.
- All mappings between Irodori/Minna and LatSol unit IDs; they remain candidates.
- LatSol internal 23 competency prerequisites and F0–F5 labels are editorial design hypotheses, not official textbook progression.
- Accuracy of Japanese content, suitability of assessment, JFT-Basic A2 equivalence, browser runtime behavior.
