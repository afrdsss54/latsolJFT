# S7.0 — Curriculum Alignment Audit (2026-11-23)

## Status verifikasi
- **Struktur resmi diverifikasi**: Minna Shokyu edisi kedua I/II = 25 + 25 pelajaran; Irodori Starter/Elementary 1/Elementary 2 = 18 × 3 pelajaran.
- **Judul Irodori**: hanya yang benar-benar diperiksa di halaman daftar pelajaran resmi yang ditandai `official-lesson-title`; sisanya tetap null.
- **Minna per-bab**: struktur 50 bab terverifikasi, tetapi urutan pola grammar dan tujuan per bab **BELUM** diverifikasi terhadap daftar learning points edisi kedua. Topic keywords warisan S4.0 adalah hipotesis.
- **Irodori Can-do**: daftar aktivitas dan rumusan Can-do per pelajaran **BELUM** diaudit seluruhnya; jangan mengklaim alignment detail.
- **Pencocokan**: candidate IDs dibuat lewat kesamaan kata kunci; ini daftar untuk pemeriksa manusia, bukan equivalence.
- **Hak cipta**: tidak menyalin dialog, latihan, atau halaman buku. Judul singkat dipakai hanya untuk identifikasi referensi.

## Prioritas gap
- **G01 P0 — F3-F5 seven-step**: Unit F3=0, F4=0, F5=0. Tindakan: Rancang unit can-do orisinal F3–F5 setelah validasi referensi (S7.3).
- **G02 P0 — Irodori per-activity Can-do**: 54 lesson exist; lesson-level titles only partially verified; per-activity Can-do not fully inspected. Tindakan: Verifikasi tabel Can-do tiap pelajaran terhadap PDF resmi (S7.1).
- **G03 P0 — Minna edition-specific lesson grammar**: 50 lesson existence verified; exact lesson-by-lesson grammar sequence not verified. Tindakan: Review table of key learning points for exact 2nd edition (S7.1).
- **G04 P1 — Kana coverage**: F0 kana content exists but complete gojuon/dakuten/yoon evaluation not established. Tindakan: Audit full kana inventory and author missing original drills (S7.2).
- **G05 P1 — Competency mastery validity**: Listening TTS and speaking self-check cannot prove real pronunciation or comprehension mastery. Tindakan: Design multiple independent item forms and evidence rubric (S7.2–S7.4).
- **G06 P1 — Editorial review**: Japanese content and one numeric-dialogue inconsistency previously flagged. Tindakan: Native/expert proofread and fix flagged issue (S7.4).
- **G07 P1 — CBT alignment**: 500 question bank validated structurally, not officially blueprint-mapped item-by-item. Tindakan: Map question skill/Can-do by reviewer without altering CBT (S7.4).
- **G08 P1 — Prerequisite validation**: S6 heuristic recommendations and unit prerequisites have no independent pedagogy review. Tindakan: Review dependency DAG and prerequisite test coverage (S7.1).
