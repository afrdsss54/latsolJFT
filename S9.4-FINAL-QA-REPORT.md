# LatSol S9.4 Mega Smart Examination 2.0 — Final QA

Baseline: LatSol-JFT-JLS-S9.3-Mega-Final-Clean.zip. Status: development checkpoint; **NOT Production Stable**.

## Actual delivery
- New bank: 546 items (390 final, 156 practice), 13 chapters BAB 02–14; every chapter has 30 final items and 12 practice items. Final: 10 vocabulary, 12 grammar, 8 reading; practice: 4 of each. 36 final items are typed Japanese, other 510 are multiple-choice. 78 distinct short reading stimuli, each used for two questions in its own pool. Vocabulary options assembled from other terms in the same chapter and same pool; all prompts, reading passages, answer keys and grammar contexts authored for this release. No external official exam materials copied. Common vocabulary and patterns can overlap existing study material.
- Separate practice modes (basic vocabulary, intermediate grammar, advanced reading, four questions each), 12-item PR, 30-item final (35 minutes), remedial drawn only from the practice pool for skills missed in the most recent final. Modes share practice cards; not independently calibrated difficulty levels. Retakes shuffle the same finite bank and options; no hidden server bank. Final timing and 90/100 threshold are internal LatSol design choices, not official JFT rules.
- Score = rounded correct/total*100; pass decision uses unrounded ratio >= .90 (27/30). Wrong or unanswered counts as zero; equal weight; no partial credit. Japanese inputs accept explicit alternatives plus NFKC, whitespace and terminal punctuation normalization. No semantic AI grading. Score reports, per-skill breakdown, answer review and full completed session history. Mastery is sticky once a final is passed; latest failure still triggers weakness recommendation. PR and remedial cannot grant final mastery.
- User can move between questions, revise answers, clear a response and submit. Final timer uses wall-clock deadline and automatically submits while session stays open. Cancellation/reload/navigation away discards in-progress answers; no active-session recovery. History saved after submit only. Client-side self-study, not proctored or secure certification: answers are inspectable in source.
- New key `latsoljft-learning-s94:<encoded profile>` uses existing Unified Storage adapter. No migration of old mastery because old 4-question exam differs from new 30-item exam. Legacy exam remains reachable in BAB menu and new exam menu. Adaptive Path 3.0 adds 13 exam94 evidence rows and separately labels latest mistakes S9.4. Planner estimates 35 minutes for final; shorter budget can omit it; review queue links to exam menu, not auto-start. No combined mastery score.

## Content QA and limitations
- Structural validation passes, not independent linguistic certification. Reviewed authored answer keys and representative grammar/counter/reading contexts; some ambiguous expressions and legitimate variants can remain. Explicit Japanese typing alternatives do not cover all possible spellings. Reading explanation identifies the answer and points to passage rather than providing complete Japanese translation or romaji. 78 reading passages yield 156 reading questions; not 156 distinct passages. No full semantic duplicate comparison with every historical lesson/CBT item. No official Minna/Irodori or JLPT/JFT alignment verification. No listening, speaking or free-response translation examination in new bank. No random sampling from more than 30 final items per chapter yet; retake memorization is possible. No item-level difficulty calibration. Old exams are not a separate additional source of new-bank mastery.
- Old data remains as-is; only simulated storage tested. History can grow and exceed browser quota; failed save shown to user. Multi-tab concurrent writes remain a pre-existing storage risk. No active exam persistence; do not refresh during test. Local content/answers are not tamper-resistant. Backup before deployment; keep S9.3 ZIP for rollback.
